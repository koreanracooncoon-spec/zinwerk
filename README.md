# Zinwerk

A Dutch grammar game from B1 to C1. Open it at https://koreanracooncoon-spec.github.io/zinwerk/ and add it to your phone's home screen.

- 4 levels, 58 circles, 4 level tests, 461 questions
- English grammar notes, with comparisons to German
- Progress is saved in your browser

About half the sentences were written by native speakers on [Tatoeba](https://tatoeba.org) (CC BY 2.0 FR). Each of those questions links to its source sentence.
